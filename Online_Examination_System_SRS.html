1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this document is to analyse and specify the business and software requirements of the Online Examination System. The system is intended to conduct examinations online by managing students, examiners, question banks, examination schedules, answer submissions, automatic evaluation and result publication, thereby replacing the manual paper-based examination process.

1.2 Project Scope

The system will allow an institution to register students and examiners, create question banks, schedule examinations, conduct timed online tests, evaluate objective answers automatically, store results securely and generate performance reports. It will be implemented using a relational database with well-defined tables, keys and relationships, along with a web-based user interface for students and administrators.

1.3 Intended Users

Students - register, log in, attend scheduled examinations and view results.

Examiners / Faculty - create questions, define question papers and review results.

Administrators - manage users, schedule examinations, monitor sessions and publish results.

2. Business Requirements

The following are the key business needs the system must satisfy:

Maintain accurate and up-to-date records of students and examiners.

Maintain a subject-wise question bank with difficulty level and correct answers.

Allow administrators to schedule examinations with date, time and duration.

Conduct examinations online with a countdown timer and automatic submission.

Evaluate objective-type answers automatically and calculate marks without manual effort.

Store results securely and allow students to view their scorecards after publication.

Generate reports such as pass percentage, subject-wise performance and topper lists.

Prevent malpractice through unique login sessions and randomised question order.

3. Functional Requirements

Functional requirements describe what the system must do.

ID

Requirement Description

FR-1

The system shall allow new students to register with name, register number, email, phone number, department and password.

FR-2

The system shall authenticate students, examiners and administrators using a unique login ID and password before granting access.

FR-3

The system shall allow examiners to add, update and delete questions in the question bank along with options, correct answer, marks and difficulty level.

FR-4

The system shall allow administrators to create an examination by selecting subject, question set, total marks, date, start time and duration.

FR-5

The system shall display questions to the student one at a time or as a set, in randomised order, during the scheduled examination window.

FR-6

The system shall display a countdown timer and automatically submit the answer sheet when the allotted time expires.

FR-7

The system shall store every submitted answer against the student, question and examination.

FR-8

The system shall automatically evaluate objective answers and compute the total marks obtained.

FR-9

The system shall allow administrators to publish results, after which students can view their scorecard and pass/fail status.

FR-10

The system shall allow administrators to generate reports such as subject-wise performance, pass percentage and rank list for a given examination.

FR-11

The system shall prevent a student from attempting the same examination more than once.

FR-12

The system shall maintain an audit log of login time, submission time and IP address for each examination attempt.

4. Non-Functional Requirements

Performance: The system shall load the next question within 2 seconds and support at least 500 students writing an examination concurrently.

Security: Passwords shall be stored in hashed form; answer keys shall not be exposed to students before result publication; all traffic shall use HTTPS.

Reliability: Submitted answers shall be saved continuously so that no data is lost during a network or power failure.

Availability: The system shall be available during the entire published examination window with at least 99% uptime.

Usability: The examination screen shall be simple, distraction free and usable on both desktop and mobile devices.

Scalability: The database design shall support an increasing number of students, subjects and question banks over time.

Maintainability: Question banks and examination rules shall be configurable without changing the source code.

5.3 Key Relationships

One Student can attempt many Exams; one Exam can be attempted by many Students (many-to-many, resolved through the Result / Attempt table).

One Subject contains many Questions (one-to-many).

One Exam contains many Questions (many-to-many through an Exam_Question table).

One Exam attempt generates many Answer records (one-to-many).

Each Result record is linked to exactly one Student and one Exam.

6. Assumptions and Constraints

The system will be developed using a relational database (MySQL / Oracle / PostgreSQL).

Only registered and approved students can attend examinations.

Examinations in this phase are objective (multiple-choice) type; descriptive evaluation is out of scope.

Every student is assumed to have a device with a stable internet connection and a supported browser.

Each examination attempt must be linked to exactly one student.

This document covers Week 1 requirements only; the Question Bank, Examination and Result modules will be detailed in subsequent weeks.
